# V11.4 — count-in and metronome

This local source candidate adds recording preparation controls to Song Studio. It includes the earlier local dictation and recovery milestones. Full platform builds, installers and hardware listening tests remain pending.

## Use it

1. Open **Song Studio** and set BPM and Bars as usual.
2. Select **Count-in: 1 bar** or **Count-in: 2 bars**. A bar is four quarter-note clicks. **Off**, the default, preserves immediate recording.
3. Set **Click level** to a comfortable headphone level. A zero level silences both count-in and metronome cues; the visual countdown still runs.
4. Enable **Metronome** if you want the click to continue during recording or playback. The count-in itself clicks even when this switch is off.
5. Press **Record take**. Get ready during the displayed count, then perform when the display switches to Recording, bar 1, beat 1. The first beat of every bar is accented.
6. Press **Stop** during count-in to cancel without creating a recorded take. Once recording has begun, Stop preserves the partial take as before.
7. Save the song and export the WAV normally.

The same controls are available in beat, lyrics and dictation views. Count-in length can only change while stopped. Metronome and Click level can change during transport.

## Timing and recorded audio

Count-in uses the audio sample clock and the song's BPM. It does not use a wall-clock UI timer. Fractional beat lengths are calculated from absolute sample positions, avoiding cumulative rounding drift. The countdown is outside the chosen song length: a four-bar song still records and exports four bars.

During count-in, your existing processed input remains audible and the beat has not started. At the first song sample, recording and the drum pattern begin together. The recorded track receives the processed input before drum and click mixing. The offline WAV renderer never adds the metronome.

**Use headphones.** Software exclusion does not prevent acoustic click bleed from speakers into your microphone. Physical interface latency and listening comfort still need testing with the AudioBox when it is available.

Playback always starts immediately at bar 1; count-in applies only to Record take. The live status shows bar, beat and elapsed song time. Song time stays at zero during the countdown.

## Settings and compatibility

Count-in, metronome enablement and click level are per-instance session controls. They survive panel closing/reopening within the same processor instance, but are not stored in `.idwsong`, recovery checkpoints, plugin presets or DAW state. Opening a song in the same instance keeps your current cue settings. A new instance defaults to count-in Off and metronome Off.

Song format 2 remains unchanged and readable by V11.1–V11.3. Original V11 format 1 is still accepted. Song Studio still records one processed audio track and is not synchronized to the DAW transport.

Recovery does not snapshot an active count-in or recording. Writing checkpoints continue independently. Completed takes become eligible for full recovery checkpoints after transport stops.

## Build and test

Use the [Windows source-build instructions](V11.1-UNIFIED-LYRICS.md), substituting `build-v11.4`. Build all targets and run CTest. The new target is `IDWSongCountIn`.

Local automated tests pass:

- One/two-bar boundaries at multiple tempos and sample rates, including fractional beat lengths.
- A count-in-to-record transition inside a 127-sample audio block.
- No recorded take or elapsed song time during count-in.
- Exact recorded/exported duration without the countdown.
- Cue exclusion from the saved take and exported WAV, checked against known input samples.
- Cancel/restart behavior, immediate playback, metronome Off and legacy immediate recording.

Editor/processor/test source also pass JUCE-header syntax checks. These checks do not establish installer readiness or real microphone performance.

Manual acceptance after platform builds: inspect all three panel views at minimum size; listen to first-beat accents; record with each count-in setting; cancel at different countdown points; adjust click level and metronome during transport; verify the saved/reopened take and exported WAV; and confirm the documented session-only settings behavior. AudioBox testing is deferred until the hardware is available.
