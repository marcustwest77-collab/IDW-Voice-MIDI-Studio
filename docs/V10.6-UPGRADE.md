# V10.6 — IDW Custom Skin Studio

V10.6 adds six live-switchable interface skins without changing the audio or MIDI engine.

## Built-in skins

- IDW Gold
- Midnight Studio
- Electric Blue
- Vocal Heat
- Platinum
- High Contrast

Choose a skin from the **SKIN** selector in the top toolbar. The change is immediate and is stored in the plugin/standalone state, so DAW projects reopen with their selected appearance. The selector is available in both Performance View and Studio Controls.

The skin controls the main background, cards, knobs, buttons, meters, pitch trace, text hierarchy, Vocal FX panel, Studio Instrument panel, and Setup/Help panel. High Contrast uses a black background, white primary controls, and a bright green signal color for improved visibility.

Skins are visual only. Switching skins does not stop audio, reset presets, alter MIDI routing, change vocal processing, or add measurable audio-thread work.
