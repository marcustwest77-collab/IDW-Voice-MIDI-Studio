# IDW Voice MIDI Studio V10.3 — Adaptive Vocal Engine

V10.3 improves musical feel and makes vocal-effect comparisons more dependable without changing the proven V10 voice-to-MIDI path.

## Added

- **Adaptive Tune**: target-note changes use a short correction time while held notes use gentler correction.
- **Vibrato Preserve**: controls how strongly Adaptive Tune protects small pitch movement around a sustained note.
- **Level Match**: a slow, bounded loudness follower that reduces the “louder sounds better” bias when comparing the production rack against bypass.
- **Tempo-synced delay**: 1/8, dotted 1/8, 1/4 and 1/2 divisions calculated from the main BPM control.
- Vocal presets now activate Adaptive Tune, choose an appropriate vibrato value, and enable Level Match.
- New native regression coverage for adaptive timing, smoothing, tempo delay and Level Match stability.

## Suggested starting points

- Natural: Adaptive Tune on, Vibrato 85%, Retune 95 ms.
- Smooth R&B: Vibrato 75%, light doubler/reverb, dotted-1/8 delay when wanted.
- Tight/Singing Rap: Vibrato 28–35%, faster Retune.
- Memphis Hard/Robot: minimal vibrato protection for deliberate hard correction.

## Compatibility and limits

Adaptive Tune, the rack and Level Match default off when V10.2 or older sessions are loaded. The tuner remains monophonic. V10.3 does not claim formant preservation and does not include AAX. Pro Tools Intro continues to use standalone + loopMIDI and the Audio Lab WAV workflow.
