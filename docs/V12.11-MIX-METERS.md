# V12.11 — track meters and held mix peaks

This local source candidate adds level feedback to Song Studio's Timeline. Native GUI, installers and AudioBox validation remain pending.

## Check your mix

1. Open **Song Studio > Timeline**.
2. Click **Reset peaks** at the top left of the timeline ruler.
3. Play the section or whole song you want to check. Each track shows a held peak in dBFS and, where row height permits, a moving bar spanning −60 to 0 dBFS. Silence shows −∞.
4. Watch **Beat peak** and **Mix peak** in the header. If **LIMIT** appears, the combined signal crossed the safety limiter's knee (0.94 linear, approximately −0.54 dBFS).
5. Stop, lower the relevant track gain or master track/beat level, apply track edits, reset peaks and replay. Existing mixer edits still require stopped transport.

A positive track peak turns red and indicates that track's contribution exceeded full scale before limiting. Several individually quiet tracks can also combine into a high mix peak. These are diagnostic indicators; the output safety limiter still processes audio as before.

## What is measured

- **Track:** the combined audible clips after trims, fades, per-track gain/pan, mute/solo and the track master level. During recording, monitored input is included on the armed track; existing backing on that track is also included.
- **Beat:** the synthesized drum pattern after beat gain and solo suppression.
- **Mix:** the combined monitored signal immediately before the output safety limiter, including enabled metronome or count-in cues.

The track reading is the higher absolute sample peak across the output channels. Mono playback measures the actual mono sum, including cancellation between channels. Unprocessed microphone levels and sound from unrelated host tracks are not represented. During count-in, only monitored input and cue activity are shown.

Turn off the metronome for music-only level checks. Export excludes cues and has its own rendering snapshot, so offline exports do not alter live meter readings. Metering does not change source audio, song data or project-file formats.

## Peak holds and limits

The UI polls interval peaks at 10 Hz; audio blocks retain short peaks between polls. Moving bars decay visually while held readings keep their maxima until Reset peaks or the Song Studio panel is destroyed. Stop, seek and loading another song do not automatically clear the holds, so reset before each comparison. Closing just the Timeline tab does not clear them.

These are sample-peak meters, not true-peak, LUFS, RMS or calibrated loudness meters. LIMIT indicates that the pre-limiter signal exceeded the processing threshold; it does not quantify gain reduction or prove that a rendered file clipped. Meter state is not saved in songs or presets.

Meter collection uses fixed stack arrays and lock-free atomics. No allocation or extra source-buffer copy is added to the audio callback for metering. Hardware CPU/latency testing is still required before release.

## Build and run on Windows

With Visual Studio 2022 C++ and CMake, from the repository:

```bat
cmake -S . -B build-v12.11 -G "Visual Studio 17 2022" -A x64 -DBUILD_TESTING=ON
cmake --build build-v12.11 --config Release
ctest --test-dir build-v12.11 -C Release --output-on-failure
"build-v12.11\IDWVoiceMIDIStudio_artefacts\Release\Standalone\IDW Voice MIDI Studio.exe"
```

Configuration fetches pinned JUCE or accepts `-DIDW_JUCE_PATH=...`. Audio Lab packaging is separate. This milestone does not update an installed Windows copy.

## Validation

`IDWSongMeters` exercises gain/pan and master levels, mute/solo, interval exchange/reset, isolation from offline export, mono recording, count-in cues and overload measurement before limiting. Recording and stem-export regressions check that the shared audio path still behaves correctly. Editor/processor syntax checks cover the UI integration.

Release gates: native minimum-size/all-skins visual inspection, reset/decay behavior while playing and stopped, comparison against audible output, AudioBox recording/latency/CPU checks, plugin validation and installer testing.
